**Generate Sales** covers everything from setting up customers and pricing to creating orders and managing your sales team. This section walks through the domain concepts you need to understand before selling in OpenMRP.

### Customer setup

Before creating customers, configure the building blocks that control how customers behave:

- **<InternalLink pathKey="generateSales.customerSetup.customerGroups" text="Customer groups" />**: classify customers by segment (type groups) and control pricing eligibility (pricing groups)
- **<InternalLink pathKey="generateSales.customerSetup.shippingTerms" text="Shipping terms" />**: define who pays freight and how charges are calculated
- **<InternalLink pathKey="generateSales.customerSetup.paymentTerms" text="Payment terms" />**: define when and how customers pay
- **<InternalLink pathKey="generateSales.customerSetup.priorities" text="Priorities" />**: control order-processing precedence in fulfillment
- **<InternalLink pathKey="generateSales.customerSetup.accountStatuses" text="Account statuses" />**: control whether a customer can place orders and receive shipments
- **<InternalLink pathKey="generateSales.customerSetup.exemptions" text="Commission & freight exemption" />**: exclude customers or groups from commission and freight charges
- **<InternalLink pathKey="generateSales.customerSetup.defaultSalesReps" text="Default sales reps" />**: assign the team member automatically added to new orders
- **<InternalLink pathKey="generateSales.customerSetup.defaultCarriers" text="Default carriers & options" />**: set the shipping carrier and service level for a customer's orders
- **<InternalLink pathKey="generateSales.customerSetup.addresses" text="Addresses" />**: define billing and shipping locations

### Customer pricing

Control what each customer pays through layered pricing:

- **<InternalLink pathKey="generateSales.customerPricing.customerPrices" text="Customer prices" />**: product-line-specific pricing that overrides the base product price
- **<InternalLink pathKey="generateSales.customerPricing.volumeDiscounts" text="Volume discounts" />**: tiered percentage discounts based on order quantity
- **<InternalLink pathKey="generateSales.customerPricing.discountCodes" text="Discount codes" />**: promotional codes that reduce the order total
