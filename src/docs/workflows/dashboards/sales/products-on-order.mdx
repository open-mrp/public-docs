The **products on order** dashboard shows the current state of all open orders. It surfaces which products are most ordered, which are back-ordered, and lets you drill into individual orders to see line-level detail.

### Why products on order matters

- **Back-order visibility**: quickly see which products have unfulfilled quantities so you can prioritize production or purchasing
- **Order status at a glance**: summary cards show total ordered, back-ordered, and fulfilled counts across all open orders
- **Drill-down detail**: expand any order row to see the exact SKUs, quantities, and fulfillment status per line item

### What you'll see

#### Summary cards

Three metric cards displayed at the top of the page:

| Card             | What it shows                                 |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Ordered**      | Total quantity ordered across all open orders |
| **Back ordered** | Total quantity still unfulfilled              |
| **Fulfilled**    | Total quantity already shipped                |

#### Top products

A scrollable list of products sorted by quantity back-ordered. Each entry shows the SKU, description, quantity back-ordered, and fulfilled vs ordered counts with the unit of measure.

#### Open orders table

An expandable table listing every open order. Each row shows the order number, status (Issued or In Progress), customer name, customer number, ship-to location, item count, and total ordered.

Click the expand icon on any row to reveal the **order details** table with per-line-item columns: SKU, description, unit price, quantity back-ordered, quantity shipped, and total ordered.

### Filters

| Field               | What it does                            |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **Customers**       | Limits data to selected customers       |
| **Customer groups** | Limits data to selected customer groups |
| **Product lines**   | Limits data to selected product lines   |
| **Sales reps**      | Limits data to selected sales reps      |

### Export

An **Export** button downloads the open orders data as an Excel file.

### Role-based visibility

Sales reps cannot filter by sales rep. The filter is hidden for users with a sales rep role. The exported data format also differs for sales reps.
