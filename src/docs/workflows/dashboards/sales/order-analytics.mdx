The **order analytics** dashboard is the primary sales performance view. It combines profit metrics, trend charts, best-seller rankings, and distribution breakdowns into a single page so you can evaluate revenue performance across customers, products, and reps.

### Why order analytics matters

- **Measure profitability**: see sales, cost, profit, and gross margin at a glance with period-over-period comparison
- **Spot trends**: cumulative sales charts show whether revenue is tracking ahead or behind the comparison period
- **Identify top performers**: best-selling products and customer sales tables surface where your revenue is concentrated
- **Segment revenue**: distribution charts break down sales by product line, customer type, sales rep, and discount to reveal how revenue is distributed

### What you'll see

#### Profit overview

A set of summary cards displayed across the top of the page:

| Card             | What it shows                                                              |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sales**        | Total invoiced revenue for the period, with comparison to the prior period |
| **Cost**         | Total cost of goods sold                                                   |
| **Profit**       | Revenue minus cost                                                         |
| **Gross margin** | Profit as a percentage of revenue                                          |

#### Cumulative sales revenue

An area chart plotting cumulative revenue over time for both the current and comparison periods. This makes it easy to see whether you are tracking ahead or behind.

#### Best selling products

A scrollable, ranked list of products sorted by total invoiced value. Each entry shows quantity sold, unit price, and percent change from the comparison period.

#### Customer revenue by segment

An area chart breaking revenue into new-customer and established-customer cohorts over time. A "new customer" is an account created within one week of its first order.

#### Customer sales table

A table listing every customer with their current-period and comparison-period sales totals and percent change. A totals row at the bottom sums all values.

#### Invoices overview

A table of individual invoices showing invoice number, customer name, customer number, ship-to location, item count, and total sold.

#### Distribution charts

Four donut charts with accompanying sortable tables:

- **Sales by product line**
- **Sales by customer type**
- **Sales by sales rep**
- **Sales by discount**

Each chart includes comparison-period data so you can see shifts in distribution.

### Filters

| Field                     | What it does                                                                    |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Date range**            | Sets the current analysis period                                                |
| **Comparison date range** | Sets the period to compare against (defaults to the same period one year prior) |
| **Customers**             | Limits data to selected customers                                               |
| **Customer groups**       | Limits data to selected customer groups                                         |
| **Product lines**         | Limits data to selected product lines                                           |
| **Sales reps**            | Limits data to selected sales reps                                              |

### Export

An **Export** button downloads the sales analytics data as an Excel file.

### Role-based visibility

Sales reps see a simplified view:

- The **Cost**, **Profit**, and **Gross margin** cards are replaced with **Sales target** and **Progress** cards showing progress toward the rep's target
- The **Sales by sales rep** and **Customer revenue by segment** charts are hidden
- The **Sales reps** filter is not available
- Exported data is scoped to the rep's own sales
