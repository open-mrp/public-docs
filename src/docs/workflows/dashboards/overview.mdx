**Dashboards** provide a suite of analytics views that surface the metrics you need to run your business. Each dashboard focuses on a specific area, from sales performance and delivery tracking to accounts receivable and demand forecasting.

### Sales

Track revenue, orders, and product performance:

- **<InternalLink pathKey="dashboards.sales.orderAnalytics" text="Order analytics" />**: profit overview, cumulative sales, best sellers, customer revenue by segment, and sales distribution charts
- **<InternalLink pathKey="dashboards.sales.orderData" text="Order data" />**: raw order line-level data grid with Excel export
- **<InternalLink pathKey="dashboards.sales.quarterlyOrders" text="Quarterly orders" />**: order quantity trends by quarter across multiple years
- **<InternalLink pathKey="dashboards.sales.productsOnOrder" text="Products on order" />**: open orders overview with top ordered and back-ordered products
- **<InternalLink pathKey="dashboards.sales.weeksOfSales" text="Weeks of sales" />**: weekly aggregated sales data over a configurable period

### Operations

Monitor delivery performance, production efficiency, and material needs:

- **<InternalLink pathKey="dashboards.operations.deliveryAnalytics" text="Delivery analytics" />**: on-time delivery percentage, average delivery time, and first shipment time
- **<InternalLink pathKey="dashboards.operations.manufacturingAnalytics" text="Manufacturing analytics" />**: production units, costs per unit, margin, quality, and labor efficiency
- **<InternalLink pathKey="dashboards.operations.materialAnalytics" text="Material analytics" />**: supplier and material cost/usage data with reorder indicators

### Financial

Stay on top of what customers owe and how payments are applied:

- **<InternalLink pathKey="dashboards.financial.accountsReceivable" text="Accounts receivable" />**: aging report with current, 30, 60, 90, and 120-day buckets
- **<InternalLink pathKey="dashboards.financial.paymentsData" text="Payments data" />**: payment allocations with invoice vs balance view

### Forecasting & targets

Plan ahead with data-driven projections and goal tracking:

- **<InternalLink pathKey="dashboards.forecasting.demandForecast" text="Demand forecast" />**: demand, revenue, and sales forecast charts with confidence intervals
- **<InternalLink pathKey="dashboards.forecasting.salesTargets" text="Sales targets" />**: sales rep target tracking and management
