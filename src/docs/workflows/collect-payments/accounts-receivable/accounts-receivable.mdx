**Accounts receivable** gives you visibility into what customers owe, how long balances have been outstanding, and the tools to collect. It ties together <InternalLink pathKey="collectPayments.invoicing.overview" text="invoicing" /> and <InternalLink pathKey="collectPayments.collecting.overview" text="collecting" /> into a single view of your open balances.

### AR dashboard

The main accounts receivable page shows all customers with outstanding balances. Each customer row displays the total amount owed broken down by aging buckets, giving you a quick picture of where collections stand across your entire customer base.

### Aging buckets

Outstanding invoices are grouped into aging buckets based on how many days have passed since the invoice date:

| Bucket       | Days outstanding |
| ------------ | ---------------- |
| **Current**  | 0 – 30 days      |
| **Over 30**  | 31 – 60 days     |
| **Over 60**  | 61 – 90 days     |
| **Over 90**  | 91 – 120 days    |
| **Over 120** | 121+ days        |

Aging is calculated from the invoice date, not the due date.

### Customer-specific AR

Drill into a single customer to see their individual unpaid invoices with an aging breakdown. The customer view also shows open credits (unallocated transaction amounts) and totals, giving you a complete picture of that customer's receivable position.

### Effective date

The AR dashboard supports an **effective date** picker that lets you view aging as of a specific historical date. This is useful for month-end reporting or investigating what balances looked like at a particular point in time.

### Emailing statements

Send a **Statement of Account** email to customers containing an Excel attachment with all unpaid invoices and their aging breakdown. You can specify multiple recipient email addresses when sending a statement, making it easy to notify both the customer's AP team and your internal contacts.

### Exporting data

Accounts receivable data can be exported in two formats:

- **Aging report (Excel)**: a summary by customer showing balances in each aging bucket
- **Invoice data (CSV)**: raw invoice-level data for further analysis or import into other systems

### Credit hold

When a customer's balance becomes a concern, you can place them on **credit hold** to prevent further orders or shipments until the situation is resolved. There are two hold types:

- **Hold All**: blocks both order issuance and shipment
- **Hold Shipment**: blocks shipment only, allowing orders to still be entered

Credit hold is set manually on the customer record and takes effect immediately on all in-progress orders.

### Collection tools summary

Augno provides several tools to help manage and collect outstanding balances:

- Email statements to customers with aging breakdowns
- Email individual invoices as PDF attachments
- Mark invoices as paid through settlements
- Settle payments, credits, adjustments, and rebates to invoices
- Put customers on credit hold to block orders or shipments
- Export aging data for reporting and analysis
- Track open credits for future allocation
