Your **profile** contains the personal information associated with your user account. These settings are visible to other team members and appear throughout the dashboard wherever your name is shown.

### Why your profile matters

- **Identity**: your display name and email identify you across the dashboard, in team lists, and on activity logs
- **Communication**: your email address is used for notifications, password resets, and account recovery
- **Recognition**: your profile photo helps team members identify you at a glance

### Display name

Your display name appears in the team members list, activity logs, and anywhere the system references your user. Update it from the **General** tab in <InternalLink pathKey="frontend.account" text="account settings" />.

### Email address

Your email address is used for login and notifications. Changing your email updates where all future communications are sent.

### Profile photo

Upload a profile photo to personalize your account. The crop editor lets you adjust the visible area before saving. Supported formats include JPEG and PNG.

### Current role

Your profile displays your current role (e.g., Admin, Viewer). This is a read-only field — only an admin can change your role from the <InternalLink pathKey="manageAccount.team.teamMembers" text="team members" /> page.
