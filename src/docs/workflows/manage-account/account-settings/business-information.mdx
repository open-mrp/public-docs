**Business information** defines your organization's identity within Augno. These details appear on the customer portal, outgoing communications, and across the dashboard. Only admins can edit business information.

### Why business information matters

- **Branding**: your account logo and name represent your organization throughout the system and on customer-facing pages
- **Customer communication**: support email, phone, and website give your customers a way to reach you
- **Portal presence**: social links and portal slug shape your public-facing customer portal

### Account identity

- **Account name**: your organization's display name, shown in the dashboard header and customer portal
- **Account logo**: a logo image displayed alongside your account name; supports JPEG and PNG with a crop editor

### Contact details

Configure the contact information your customers see:

- **Support email**: the primary email address for customer inquiries
- **Phone number**: your organization's support or main phone line
- **Website URL**: a link to your company website
- **Headquarters address**: your organization's physical address

### Social links

Add social media handles to connect your online presence:

- **Instagram**
- **Facebook**
- **LinkedIn**
- **Twitter / X**

These links appear on your customer portal and help customers find your business on social platforms.

### Portal slug

The portal slug is a URL-safe identifier used in your customer portal URL. Choose a slug that's short, memorable, and matches your brand.
