The **Support** tab in account settings decides who hears from your customers. It points inbound portal support at a **group conversation**: when a customer opens a support case, the group's members are seated on that case as participants, so the message has a definite recipient instead of landing in an unattended queue.

The Support tab is admin-only in the dashboard, and the underlying operations require messaging permission on your own account — admins always qualify. Customer and supplier portal accounts can never configure it.

### Why it matters

**Support routing is what makes the portal's Contact support button exist.** The <InternalLink pathKey="generateSales.customerPortal" text="customer portal" /> checks support availability before offering the action, and a customer's first case is refused unless their scope resolves to a route whose group has at least one active member. An account that never configures a route ships a portal where support simply appears to be missing — nothing is broken, the feature is gated on this setting.

### Setting the route

1. Create the group chat first, in **Messages**, and add the people who should handle support to it. Only group conversations can be a support route — a direct message cannot, and neither can a conversation belonging to another account.
2. Open the **Support** tab and pick that group from the **Group conversation** list. The tab lists your account's group chats; if there are none yet, it tells you to create one in Messages first.
3. Save. The tab then shows the current group and lists its active members as the recipients.

Manage **who** receives support by adding and removing people from the group chat itself — there is no separate recipient list on this tab. Only active human members of the group are seated on new cases.

Use **Remove** to clear the route. Clearing it leaves any customer **without an override of its own** unable to open new support cases until a route is set again; customers covered by a per-customer override keep using that override.

### Scopes: default and per-customer override

A route is stored against a scope. Two scopes exist:

- **Account default** — used for any customer that has no override of its own. This is what the Support tab configures.
- **Per-customer override** — a route attached to one specific customer account, which wins over the default for that customer.

When a customer contacts support, Augno resolves the override first and falls back to the account default. The Support tab manages the account default; per-customer overrides exist in the routing model but are not exposed in the tab today.

### Changing a route later

Re-pointing or clearing a route only affects cases opened **from then on**. People already seated on an open case stay seated, and existing threads keep working — so moving support to a new team does not silently orphan conversations that are already in flight, and it does not retroactively expose old cases to the new team either.

### Where support routing connects

- **<InternalLink pathKey="generateSales.customerPortal" text="Customer portal" />**: the Contact support action is gated on a resolvable route
- **<InternalLink pathKey="communicate.inbox" text="Inbox" />**: the cases customers open are worked from your team's inbox

**Preview**: the support-routing endpoints are internal-only and in preview. They are not published in the public OpenAPI spec or the SDKs, and they may change without notice — configure support routing from the dashboard.
