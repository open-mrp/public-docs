**Build Products** covers everything from purchasing raw materials through manufacturing, production tracking, and inventory management.

### Items

Define the objects you buy, make, and sell. Items are the foundation for every other workflow.

- **<InternalLink pathKey="buildProducts.items.overview" text="Items overview" />**: materials, parts, and products, plus units, unit groups, and categories
- **<InternalLink pathKey="buildProducts.items.materials" text="Materials" />**: raw inputs purchased from vendors
- **<InternalLink pathKey="buildProducts.items.parts" text="Parts" />**: intermediate items manufactured during production
- **<InternalLink pathKey="buildProducts.items.products" text="Products" />**: finished goods you price, stock, and sell

### Purchasing

Procure materials from suppliers and track deliveries through to inventory.

- **<InternalLink pathKey="buildProducts.purchasing.suppliers" text="Suppliers" />**: vendor records and supplier-material links
- **<InternalLink pathKey="buildProducts.purchasing.purchaseOrders" text="Purchase orders" />**: order lifecycle from estimate through fulfillment
- **<InternalLink pathKey="buildProducts.purchasing.deliveriesAndReceiving" text="Deliveries & receiving" />**: track arrivals and stock materials into inventory

### BOMs

Define your manufacturing process with production steps instead of traditional bills of materials.

- **<InternalLink pathKey="buildProducts.bom.productionSteps" text="Production steps" />**: the core building block: inputs, outputs, labor, and overhead per stage
- **<InternalLink pathKey="buildProducts.bom.consumptionsAndProductions" text="Consumptions & productions" />**: what goes in and what comes out at each step
- **<InternalLink pathKey="buildProducts.bom.productionFlows" text="Production flows" />**: the connected sequence of steps from raw materials to finished products
- **<InternalLink pathKey="buildProducts.bom.costing" text="Costing (COGS)" />**: real-time cost of goods sold from step definitions

### Production

Execute manufacturing with production runs, batches, and batch operations.

- **<InternalLink pathKey="buildProducts.production.productionRuns" text="Production runs" />**: coordinated manufacturing executions linked to sales orders
- **<InternalLink pathKey="buildProducts.production.batches" text="Batches" />**: physical units tracked through production with quantity and quality grading
- **<InternalLink pathKey="buildProducts.production.batchOperations" text="Batch operations" />**: initialize, move, split, and merge actions that advance batches

### Scanning

Track batches on the production floor with scanning stations and labels.

- **<InternalLink pathKey="buildProducts.scanning.scanningStations" text="Scanning stations" />**: configured operation points linked to production steps
- **<InternalLink pathKey="buildProducts.scanning.labels" text="Labels" />**: batch identification labels in two formats and four sizes

### Inventory

Track every unit through receipts, issues, and allocations with full audit trail.

- **<InternalLink pathKey="buildProducts.inventory.inventory" text="Inventory overview" />**: receipts, issues, allocations, and available-to-promise
- **<InternalLink pathKey="buildProducts.inventory.storageAndLots" text="Storage locations & lots" />**: hierarchical storage and batch-level traceability
- **<InternalLink pathKey="buildProducts.inventory.changeLogs" text="Inventory change logs" />**: complete audit trail of every movement
- **<InternalLink pathKey="buildProducts.inventory.reconciliation" text="Reconciliation & bulk updates" />**: correct counts when system and reality diverge
