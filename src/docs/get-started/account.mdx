You can begin using your Augno account in testing environments as soon as you create it. In a sandbox, you can simulate using all of Augno's features without impacting production data or incurring live charges. After you activate your account, you can integrate services, manage projects, invite team members, and configure your organization's settings.

Augno generates a unique ID for your account. This ID is distinct from your account's display name and is used to uniquely identify your account in API calls and support interactions. You can typically find your account ID in your Account or Profile settings within the <InternalLink pathKey="frontend.dashboard" text="Augno Dashboard" />.
