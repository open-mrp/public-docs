When you create your OpenMRP account, we spin up a testing environment for you with seeded data. We call these testing environments, _**sandboxes**_. In a sandbox, you can simulate using all of OpenMRP's features without impacting production data or pestering your customers. After you activate your account, you can integrate services, invite team members, and configure your organization's settings.
