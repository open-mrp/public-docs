You can begin using your Augno account in testing environments as soon as you create it. In a sandbox, you can simulate using all of Augno's features without impacting production data or pestering your customers. After you activate your account, you can integrate services, invite team members, and configure your organization's settings.

Augno generates a unique ID for your account. This ID is distinct from your account's display name and is used to uniquely identify your account in API calls and support interactions.
