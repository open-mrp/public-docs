Complete this checklist before extensively using your Augno account with live data or critical integrations.

The items in this checklist apply to all Augno accounts. We also have checklists for <InternalLink pathKey="goLive" text="taking your integration live" />.
For the safety and security of your Augno account, follow these steps:

<DocChecklistItem id="review-public-info">
    **Review Public Business Information**: Ensure any public-facing information linked to your
    Augno account (e.g., business name, contact details if shared through integrations) is accurate
    and professional. Incorrect or outdated information can lead to confusion for your customers or
    partners. Review these details in your account or organization settings in the{' '}
    <InternalLink pathKey="frontend.dashboard" text="Augno Dashboard" />.
</DocChecklistItem>

<DocChecklistItem id="configure-emails">
    **Configure Email Notifications**: Augno can notify you of important account activity by email.
    You can choose which events you want to be notified about in your communication preferences,
    typically found in your account settings. If multiple team members have access to your account,
    each can often set their own notification preferences. At a minimum, we recommend turning on
    emails for critical alerts and important account updates.
</DocChecklistItem>

<DocChecklistItem id="data-integrity">
    **Establish Data Integrity and Issue Management Practices**: Data accuracy is crucial. While
    Augno provides tools to help maintain data integrity, we recommend you establish processes to: -
    Regularly review key data within the{' '}
    <InternalLink pathKey="frontend.dashboard" text="Augno Dashboard" />. - Report any anomalies or
    suspicious activity you observe using the Dashboard or API, if applicable. - Be prepared to
    provide relevant information or context if issues or discrepancies arise with your integrations
    or data. - Understand how to prevent and mitigate common data quality issues relevant to your
    use case.
</DocChecklistItem>

<DocChecklistItem id="team-permissions">
    **Set Up Team Members and Permissions**: If you work with a team, you can often give them access
    to your Augno account. Augno may allow you to assign different permissions to team members based
    on their roles and responsibilities. - When granting access, ensure each team member uses their
    own unique credentials. Do not share your login details. - Encourage all team members to enable
    2FA on their accounts. - If a team member no longer requires access, promptly remove them from
    your account through the team management section in the{' '}
    <InternalLink pathKey="frontend.dashboard" text="Augno Dashboard" />.
</DocChecklistItem>

<DocChecklistItem id="review-policy">
    **Review Augno's Acceptable Use Policy**: Familiarize yourself with Augno's terms of service and
    acceptable use policy (often found in the legal section of the Augno website). This will help
    you understand any restrictions or guidelines for using Augno services and ensure your intended
    use complies with these policies. If you have questions about specific use cases, contact Augno
    support.
</DocChecklistItem>

</DocChecklist>
